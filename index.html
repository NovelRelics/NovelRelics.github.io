Understand the Novel Relics channel.

Channel Insights Local is a personal Windows application used by the owner of the Novel Relics YouTube channel. It prepares local analytics reports to help the owner review channel performance and plan Shorts and long-form video content.

What the application does

With the channel owner's authorization, the application reads YouTube Analytics reports and YouTube channel and video information. Reports may include daily views, watch time, engagement, traffic sources, audience retention and video metadata, depending on the reports requested and the data available from Google.

Reports are saved on the owner's computer. The local video workflow may use a verified report or clearly labelled cached data as context for content planning.

Google access

The application requests only these two read-only permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

These permissions do not allow the application to upload, schedule, publish, edit or delete YouTube videos.

Privacy and control

This website provides information about the application. It does not receive OAuth credentials, tokens or private channel reports. Read the privacy policy for details about data access, storage, sharing and deletion, and the terms of service for conditions of use.
